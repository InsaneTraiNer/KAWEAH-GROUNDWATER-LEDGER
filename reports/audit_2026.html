B. Physical Distinction Between Canopy Evapotranspiration and Wellhead Extraction (Water Code §§ 10725.2, 10726.4, 10730.2 & 23 CCR § 354.18): Land IQ satellite models estimate surface energy flux and vegetative evapotranspiration (ET)—not subsurface groundwater extraction. Satellite ET cannot distinguish between groundwater pumped through a wellhead and root-zone moisture derived from winter rainfall storage, perched water tables, capillary rise, adjacent parcel wind advection, or surface canal seepage. Under SGMA (Water Code § 10726.4(a)(2)), groundwater extraction fees and penalties must bear a fair and reasonable relationship to actual groundwater extracted from the basin.
