III. Statutory & Evidentiary Legal Argument

1. CPUC Revenue-Grade Utility Metering & DWR Soundings Supersede Remote-Sensing ET Proxies

A. Admissibility and Presumption of Accuracy (Cal. Evid. Code §§ 640, 1271, 1280): PG&E 15-minute interval data is metered under CPUC Electric Tariff Rule 17 and ANSI C12.20 revenue accuracy standards (±0.2%). Concurrent groundwater depth measurements from DWR CASGEM Station 362077N1193473W001 constitute official state hydrologic records under California Evidence Code § 1280.

B. Physical Distinction Between Canopy Evapotranspiration and Wellhead Extraction (Water Code § 10726.4 & 23 CCR § 354.18): Land IQ satellite models estimate surface energy flux and vegetative evapotranspiration (ET)—not subsurface groundwater extraction. Satellite ET cannot distinguish between groundwater pumped through a wellhead and root-zone moisture derived from winter rainfall storage, perched water tables, capillary rise, adjacent parcel wind advection, or surface canal seepage. Under SGMA (Water Code § 10726.4(a)(2)), groundwater extraction fees and penalties must bear a fair and reasonable relationship to actual groundwater extracted from the basin.
